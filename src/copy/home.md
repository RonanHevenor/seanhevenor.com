Born and raised mostly in Rhode Island, I've spent more than twenty years helping people buy and sell homes here. Over that time, I've guided clients through strong markets and challenging ones. My office sits in the heart of Wickford Village, just down the road from the North Kingstown home I share with my family.
North Kingstown is a special place to live. Summer evenings bring concerts and long days at the town beach, and the sunrise over the bay never gets old. The town continues to grow with expanding businesses at Quonset and new construction around town, bringing fresh energy and new faces to the local economy.

With both the cultural and economic draws of Providence and Newport, and the South County beaches all within easy reach, it would be hard to find a better home base in Rhode Island.

I've always been fascinated by what moves the real estate market, and I enjoy helping my clients understand it. In times of uncertainty like these, that understanding makes all the difference. My goal is to help you make your next move with confidence, because I believe owning a home is financially important.

Whether you're buying, selling, or renting a home in North Kingstown, Wickford Village, or nearby southern Rhode Island communities, I bring over 20 years of local experience and a straightforward, professional approach to every transaction. If you're considering buying selling, or renting, I'd welcome the opportunity to say hello and discuss your real estate goals.
